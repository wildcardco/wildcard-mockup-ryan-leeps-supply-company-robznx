Family-run since 1954

A Region-grown plumbing wholesaler

Nick Leep started Leeps Supply in Highland, Indiana in 1954. Today it's still family-run, with counters and showrooms across the region, and it describes itself as the largest plumbing-parts supplier in Northwest Indiana.

The Lowell branch opened in 2005 to put that stock closer to the contractors working the south end of Lake County and beyond.

  • Stocked. More than 18,000 plumbing items, so the part is usually here when you need it.
  • Nearby. Counters and showrooms across the region mean less windshield time.
  • Willing to quote. Call or walk in and we'll price the whole job.
